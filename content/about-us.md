<div class="wp-block-group"><div class="wp-block-group__inner-container is-layout-flow wp-block-group-is-layout-flow"><p>We are Flo and Rico from Germany. Two folks (and good friends btw.) who just finished school and wanted to do something better than studying – traveling. So we got a little job during the summer holidays, crammed as much money as we could and well… now we are going to be on the trek for as long as we can afford. We want to visit mostly Asian countries to get the most bang for our buck but we do not yet know which countries we will see exactly and where our path will take us during the next year (or years?!). But one thing we know for sure – it is going to be awesome either way and that’s why we started this blog -&gt; to share our adventures, stories and of course our photography with the world.</p><div class="about-people">
