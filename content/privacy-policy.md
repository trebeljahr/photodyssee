This page lists every server your browser contacts when you read photodyssee.com, and what each one receives. The site has no accounts and no newsletter. It sets no cookies.

## Controller

Email for privacy questions: [imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com](mailto:imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com). The [imprint](/imprint/) has the same details.

## Servers your browser contacts

-   `photodyssee.com`: the blog itself, with its photos, fonts and scripts. Cloudflare passes each request on to a Hetzner VPS serving static files.
-   `static.cloudflareinsights.com`: the script of Cloudflare Web Analytics. Visitors in the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland do not get it.

No other server receives a request from your browser. External links contact another site only when you click them. Search matches run in your browser against a public index of the journal. New searches put your words in the URL fragment, which is not sent with the page request.

## Hosting

Every request reaches Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, first. Cloudflare passes it on to Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. The origin serves static pages, photos, fonts and scripts. It runs no WordPress, PHP or public database.

Both receive your IP address, the address of the page, the user agent and the referrer. They use them to deliver the site and to protect it against abuse.

The fonts and icons are copies on photodyssee.com. Your browser contacts no font service.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in delivering the site securely).

## Cloudflare Web Analytics

Cloudflare adds the script of [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to each page. Your browser loads it from `static.cloudflareinsights.com`, and the script sends its report to `photodyssee.com/cdn-cgi/rum`.

The report holds the page URL, the referrer and how long the page took to load. The timings include Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint and Cumulative Layout Shift. Cloudflare shows me totals by page, country, browser and device type.

The script sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It gives each page view a random ID and keeps no ID from one page view to the next. Cloudflare receives your IP address with the report, discards it at the nearest data center and does not store it.

Cloudflare does not add the script for visitors who connect through a Cloudflare data center in the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. For all other visitors the legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in knowing how fast the pages load). You can object at any time, or block `static.cloudflareinsights.com` with a content blocker.

## Former comments

Comments are closed. This static archive accepts no comments and sends no new comment data to Akismet or WordPress.com.

Private backups of the former WordPress site can contain previously submitted comments, names, email addresses, website addresses, IP addresses, user agents and submission times. These backups are not served by the public site.

Akismet and WordPress.com processed comments while the WordPress site was live. Records already held by Automattic are subject to [Automattic’s privacy policy](https://automattic.com/privacy/). Closing the site does not itself delete those records.

Email [imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com](mailto:imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com) about access to or deletion of a former comment and its associated data.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

-   access the personal data held about you (Art. 15);
-   have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
-   have your data deleted (Art. 17), unless a legal obligation requires me to keep it;
-   restrict processing (Art. 18);
-   receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
-   object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), which covers everything on this page.

To use any of these rights, email [imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com](mailto:imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com). You can also complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg.

The site makes no automated decisions about you and builds no profile of you (Art. 22 GDPR).
