This page lists every server your browser contacts when you read photodyssee.com, and what each one receives. The site has no accounts and no newsletter. Its own scripts set no cookies. Cloudflare may use cookies for security checks.

Email for privacy questions: [imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com](mailto:imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com). The [imprint](/imprint/) has the same details.

## Servers your browser contacts

-   `photodyssee.com`: the blog itself, with its photos, fonts and scripts. Cloudflare serves the static archive, including its pages and photographs.

Photos, fonts and site scripts load from this domain. Cloudflare may load resources for security checks. External links contact another site only when you click them. Search matches run in your browser against a public index of the journal. New searches put your words in the URL fragment, which is not sent with the page request.

## Hosting

Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, serves the static pages, photos, fonts and scripts. The public site runs no WordPress, PHP or database.

Cloudflare receives your IP address, the address of the page, the user agent and the referrer. It uses them to deliver the site and protect it against abuse.

The fonts and icons are copies on photodyssee.com. Your browser contacts no font service.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in delivering the site securely).

## Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is disabled. The archive contains no analytics script. Cloudflare still processes requests to deliver and protect the site.

## Former comments

Comments are closed. This static archive accepts no comments and sends no new comment data to Akismet or WordPress.com.

Private backups of the former WordPress site can contain previously submitted comments, names, email addresses, website addresses, IP addresses, user agents and submission times. These backups are not served by the public site. Private copies are retained on a Hetzner VPS and on the former Uberspace account during retirement.

Akismet and WordPress.com processed comments while the WordPress site was live. Records already held by Automattic are subject to [Automattic’s privacy policy](https://automattic.com/privacy/). Closing the site does not itself delete those records.

Email [imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com](mailto:imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com) about access to or deletion of a former comment and its associated data.

## Cookies

The archive’s own scripts set no cookies and use no browser storage. Cloudflare may set security cookies, such as `cf_clearance` after a challenge. See [Cloudflare’s cookie documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

-   access the personal data held about you (Art. 15);
-   have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
-   have your data deleted (Art. 17), unless a legal obligation requires me to keep it;
-   restrict processing (Art. 18);
-   receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
-   object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), which covers everything on this page.

To use any of these rights, email [imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com](mailto:imprint+photodyssee@trebeljahr.com). You can also complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg.

The site makes no automated decisions about you and builds no profile of you (Art. 22 GDPR).
